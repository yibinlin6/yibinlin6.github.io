You can use this type of post to write:

- course notes
- paper reading notes
- concept summaries
- self-study reflections

Suggested structure:

## Topic

State the topic or material you studied.

## Key Points

List the main ideas in your own words.

## Reflection

Write what was difficult, important, or worth reviewing again.
